About Us

History & Background

Springbok Restings was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide quality accommodation in the heart of Botswana. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a trusted destination for both local and international guests.

Mission & Values

Our mission is to deliver exceptional hospitality that makes every guest feel at home. We value comfort, integrity, and genuine care — ensuring every stay is a memorable experience.
